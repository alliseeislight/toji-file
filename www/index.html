WARM-UP (5 MIN)
  • Jumping jacks, 1 minute
  • Arm circles, 20 each way
  • Bodyweight squats, 10
  • Scapular push-ups, 10 (arms straight, just move the shoulder blades)
  • Cat-cow, 10
PROGRESSION (HOW TO GET STRONGER)
  • Every week, add 1-2 reps to each set.
  • When you hit the top of the range on every set, move to a harder version or add a loaded backpack.
  • Slow the lowering part to 3-4 seconds. It's free difficulty.
  • Rest 60-90 seconds between sets. Shorten it as you improve.
  • Find a pull-up bar and add pull-ups to Day 2 and Day 5 the day you do.
FUEL (EAT TO BUILD)
  • You're lean and light, so don't diet. Eat a bit above maintenance.
  • Aim for roughly 90-110 g of protein a day. Eggs, beans, lentils, milk, fish, chicken and groundnuts are cheap and work.
  • Small meals often suit you: 5-6 times a day.
  • Sleep 8 hours. Muscle gets built in your sleep.
  • Expect about 0.25-0.5 kg a month. Weigh yourself once a week, same time.
